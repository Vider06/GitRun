## What the refactor covered

The 1.5.0 work recorded completion of the first five roadmap phases: desktop UI/performance cleanup; implementation-versus-contract inventory; consistency and enforcement checks; core lifecycle/scaling; and component/trust-boundary mapping. It also covered the deterministic scheduler, VM resolution, GitDockRun registry checks, GSR watchdog/security hardening and the optional Kitty Smart Scheduler.

The Smart Scheduler is an optional statistical companion, not an authority over the deterministic scheduler. Its high-resolution five-minute sample window is seven days; older data is compacted into bounded weekly, four-week, 48-week and lifetime summaries. Predictive capacity changes are opt-in and bounded by operator settings and resource-pressure controls.

GitRun CI, CodeQL, DevSkim, Rustfmt and Cargo Lock Maintenance passed on the audited PR source HEAD. These results apply only to that historical commit. They do not certify later changes, an official 1.5.1 release artifact, or deployment-specific behavior.

## Known verification boundaries

The historical audit identified checks that require an actual target host or end-to-end environment, including desktop privileged authorization/install transitions, KVM/libvirt and VirtualBox provisioning/recovery, a complete authorized GitDockRun disconnect through the API, and migration of customized or pre-existing runner images/control volumes. Security alert counts must be checked separately from a successful CodeQL workflow.
